Our Humble Beginnings

Founded in 2005 by Michael Chen, Tech Communications LLC started as a small team of network specialists operating out of a modest office in Tech City, Texas. Our initial focus was providing reliable network infrastructure solutions to local businesses that were being underserved by larger IT firms.

What began as a passion project quickly grew into a respected regional provider as word spread about our personalized approach, technical expertise, and commitment to customer satisfaction.

Today, we serve clients across five states with a team of over 50 certified professionals, but we've never lost that personal touch that defined our early days.
